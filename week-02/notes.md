## In My Own Words

**What is the cybersecurity landscape, and why does it matter?**

```
The cybersecurity landscape is basically the environment we are trying to protect. It includes the technology and systems we use, the people who use them, the threats and vulnerabilities that exist, and the different ways we protect against attacks. It matters because technology and cyber threats are always changing. Understanding the landscape helps cybersecurity professionals know what risks to look for, what needs to be protected, and how to better prevent and respond to attacks.
```

**How do hardware, OS, and software layers interact?**

```
Hardware, the operating system, and software all work together in layers. Hardware is the physical part of the computer, like the CPU, memory, and storage. The operating system acts as the middle layer that manages the hardware and allows software to use those resources. Software runs on top of the operating system to perform specific tasks. Each layer depends on the others, so if there is a problem or security issue in one layer, it can affect the entire system.
```
